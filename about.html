About & Sources

Who made this, and why

Hooked by Design is a Service as Action project by four MYP 4 students. We wanted the students at our school to see how the apps they use every day are designed — and to know where the off switches are.

Who we are

MYP 4A

  • Avyukt Aggarwal
  • Neal Nikhil Suman
  • Ashvathh Sinnha
  • Atharva Kushwaha

Why we built it

In our survey, 15 of 21 students said social media mostly harms people their age — but only 5 said it harms them. 16 of 21 knew how to turn off YouTube autoplay, yet only 2 of 21 had ever used a built-in time limit.

We didn’t want to tell anyone to quit. We wanted to make the design visible — the tricks, why they work, and where the switches are — so everyone can decide for themselves.

How we built it

A plain website made with HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, hosted free on GitHub Pages. The app pictures are our own drawings, not real logos or screenshots. The demos are made up and run only in your browser.

We kept the scope to two apps, Instagram and YouTube, so we could go into detail on each one.

Our rules

No dark patterns to fight dark patterns

  • No pop-ups, guilt messages or fake urgency
  • No tracking, analytics, ads or accounts
  • The challenge saves only on your own device
  • Every demo can be skipped
  • We used one progress bar — and we point it out
