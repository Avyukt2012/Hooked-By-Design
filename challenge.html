Take back control

The 7-day challenge

One small change a day, each about two minutes. Keep what works, undo what doesn’t — this is about choosing, not quitting.

  • No account: your ticks are saved on this device only
  • No streaks: miss a day and nothing breaks

0 of 7 days done

Notice we used a progress bar here?It’s the same “unfinished task” pull that apps use on you. We think it’s fair here, because you chose this goal — but you can hide it.

Saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Day0

    Before you start: check your screen time

    Look up your average daily screen time now, so you can compare it on day 7.

    iPhone

    Settings → Screen Time → See All App & Website Activity

    Android

    Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls

    My daily average now
  2. Day1

    Turn off autoplay on YouTube

    • 02No stopping cues

    YouTube

    Open any video and tap the autoplay switch at the top of the player so it’s off. Or: You → Settings → Autoplay → turn off Autoplay next video.

    More help
  3. Day2

    Mute app notifications, keep messages from people

    • 05Triggers

    Instagram

    Profile → menu → Notifications. Turn off what isn’t from people you know, and keep messages.

    YouTube

    You → Settings → Notifications. Turn off what you don’t need, or switch on Scheduled digest. Set most channel bells to None.

    More help
  4. Day3

    Set a YouTube Shorts limit and an Instagram daily limit

    • 01Variable rewards
    • 02No stopping cues

    YouTube

    You → Settings → Time management → Shorts feed limit. Pick a time — or 0 minutes, if you have that option.

    Instagram

    Profile → menu → Time management → Daily limit.

    More help
  5. Day4

    Hide like counts on Instagram

    • 03Social validation

    Instagram

    Profile → menu → Like and share counts → turn on Hide like and share counts. For your own posts: ⋯ on the post → Hide like count.

    More help
  6. Day5

    Reset Instagram suggestions or pause YouTube watch history

    • 04Personalisation

    Instagram

    Profile → menu → Content preferences → Reset suggested content.

    YouTube

    You → Settings → Manage all history → Controls, and stop saving the videos you watch.

    More help
  7. Day6

    Turn on Instagram Sleep mode and YouTube’s bedtime reminder

    • 05Triggers
    • 02No stopping cues

    Instagram

    Profile → menu → Time management → Sleep mode, then choose your hours.

    YouTube

    You → Settings → Time management → Remind me when it’s bedtime.

    More help

    Compare your screen time with day 0, and keep what worked

    Check your screen time in the same place as on day 0.
