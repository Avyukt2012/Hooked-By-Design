What 21 students at our school told us

15 of 21 say social media mostly harms people their age. Only 5 say it harms them.

So who is it harming? Most of us think it’s everyone else. Hooked by Design shows how Instagram and YouTube are built to hold your attention, so you can spot the hooks in your own feed — and decide which ones to keep.
