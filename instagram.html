1

Endless Feed, Reels and Explore

The tricks behind it

  • 02No stopping cues
  • 01Variable rewards
  • 04Personalisation

The Feed, Reels and Explore never run out. There’s always one more post — and any swipe might be the good one.

11 of 20students in our survey said Reels kept them on longer than they planned. Another 11 said the same about the endless feed.

Take control: set a daily limit

  1. Go to your profile and tap the menu at the top right. This opens Settings and activity.
  2. Tap Time management, then Daily limit.
  3. Pick a time, from 15 minutes to 2 hours. Instagram reminds you when you reach it.

Teen accountsInstagram reminds teen accounts to leave the app after 60 minutes a day.

2

Recommendations

The trick behind it

  • 04Personalisation

Instagram learns from what you watch, like, share and linger on, then fills Explore, Reels and your Feed with more of the same.

5students asked us how to fix their feed. This is how. (And 6 of 20 said recommendations kept them on longer than planned.)

Take control: reset what it suggests

Reset suggested content

  1. Go to your profile and tap the menu .
  2. Tap Content preferences, then Reset suggested content, and follow the steps.
  3. This clears Instagram’s suggestions in Explore, Reels and your Feed. It starts learning again from what you do next — so for a few days, spend time on what you actually want more of.

See and edit your topics

In Reels, tap the Your Algorithm icon at the top right to see the topics Instagram thinks you’re into, then add or remove them. It launched in the US first, in December 2025, so it may not be in your app yet.

Quick fix for one post

Tap ⋯ on a post or reel and choose Not interested.

3

Likes and view counts

The trick behind it

  • 03Social validation

Like and view counts turn every post into a scoreboard — yours and everyone else’s.

3 of 20students said likes and views kept them on longer than planned — fewer than for Reels or Shorts, but the scoreboard is still there every time you post.

Take control: hide the numbers

On other people’s posts

  1. Go to your profile and tap the menu .
  2. Tap Like and share counts and turn on Hide like and share counts.

On your own posts

  1. Before you share: open Advanced settings and turn on Hide like and view counts on this post.
  2. Already posted? Tap ⋯ on the post, then Hide like count.

You can still see who liked a post. The number just stops being the headline.

4

Notifications

The trick behind it

  • 05Triggers

Every buzz and red badge is a reason to open the app — and once you’re in, the feed is waiting.

16 of 21students had opened an app because of a notification and stayed longer than they meant to.

Take control: fewer buzzes

Switch off the ones you don’t need

  1. Go to your profile, tap the menu , then Notifications.
  2. Turn off the kinds you don’t need — like Live, Reels and suggestions from Instagram. Keep messages from people.

Turn on Sleep mode

  1. Menu → Time management → Sleep mode.
  2. Choose the days and times. While it’s on, notifications are muted and anyone who messages you gets an automatic reply.

Good to knowSleep mode used to be called Quiet mode. Teen accounts have it on from 10 pm to 7 am — and you can schedule it for school hours too.

5

Stories

The tricks behind it

  • 06FOMO and scarcity
  • 08Unfinished tasks

Stories disappear after 24 hours, and the rings around ones you haven’t watched nag you to clear them.

3 of 20students said Stories kept them on longer than planned.

Take control: mute the habit checks

  1. Find the Story bubble of an account you only check out of habit.
  2. Press and hold it, tap Mute, then Mute story.

They aren’t told, and you can unmute them any time from their profile.

6

“Seen” in DMs

The trick behind it

  • 09Social pressure

“Seen” tells the other person you’ve read their message — so now you feel you owe them a reply, fast.

Take control: turn off read receipts

For one chat

  1. Open the chat and tap the person’s name at the top.
  2. Tap Privacy and safety and turn off Read receipts.

For all chats

  1. Go to your profile and tap the menu .
  2. Tap Messages and story replies, then Show read receipts, and switch it off.

Messages sent in vanish mode always show when they’ve been read.

Meanwhile, in the US

Some teens are getting these settings by default

In August 2026, Meta agreed with US states to change teen accounts in the US: a default 2-hour daily limit, a block from midnight to 6 am, notifications muted from 8 am to 3 pm, like counts hidden, and a switch to turn off autoplay.

These changes are not in India yet, so here you have to set them up yourself. You can get close:

  • A 2-hour daily limit Set a daily limit
  • Quiet nights, and quiet school hours Schedule Sleep mode
  • Hidden like counts Hide like and share counts
